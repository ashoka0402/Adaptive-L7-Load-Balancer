Chaos Lab

Real loopback traffic · bounded experiments

Fault injection

Change a running demo origin without touching external services.

Autoscaling

Launch real local listeners on sustained demand, then drain and remove idle replicas. Two to six servers, five-second cooldown.

Canary deployment

Allocate traffic to a separate v2 server. Auto-promote advances healthy releases through staged percentages; an open circuit or error/latency threshold triggers rollback.

Resilience controls

Toggle protections while testing. A hard concurrency ceiling always remains; disabling pooling requests connection closure.

The Breaking Point

Increase offered arrivals from 50 to 800 req/s. A step saturates when success drops below 99%, p95 exceeds 200 ms, or goodput falls below 85% of offered rate.
